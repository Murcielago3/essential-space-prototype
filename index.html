Essential Space, redesigned

Most Essential Key presses on my parents' phones were accidents they never noticed. This prototype shows three fixes: a visible capture moment with undo, a review tray for likely accidents, and search that answers the question instead of listing screenshots.

Tap through the phone. The light grey key on its right edge is the Essential Key; the dark one above it is the power key.
